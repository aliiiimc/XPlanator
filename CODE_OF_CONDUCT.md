# Code Of Conduct

XPlanator should be welcoming to beginners and experienced developers alike.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct
- Assume good intent
- Keep criticism technical and actionable
- Help beginners without talking down to them
- Respect privacy and do not ask contributors to share private code unnecessarily

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Dismissive comments toward beginners
- Sharing private code, credentials, or user data without permission
- Spam, self-promotion, or unrelated issue noise

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block users who repeatedly ignore these expectations.
